Privacy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This page explains what Skauz stores about you, what it doesn't, and which services are involved. It applies to skauz.com and the Skauz apps for Android and iOS.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, nickname, how you sign in (email, Google or Apple) together with the ID that provider gives us, a profile picture link if Google or Apple provides one, and whether your email is verified.
  • Password: only if you sign up with email, and only as a one-way hash. We never store it in plain text.
  • Observations: the species, date and time, location and any details you enter, such as count, sex, life stage, behavior and notes.
  • Contributions: replies and identifications you add to observations and species links you submit.
  • Sign-in tokens: so you stay signed in. They expire after 30 days at the latest and are removed when you sign out.

Who can see it

  • Your observations are private by default. Only you can see them.
  • If you publish an observation, other people can see it, including its location and your nickname. You can unpublish it at any time.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and we don't sell your data.
  • No location tracking. Your device's location is used to show the map around you and to fill in an observation's position. It is only saved when you save an observation.
  • No photos or audio are uploaded.
  • We never see your Google or Apple password.

On your device

  • Website: cookies that keep you signed in, a short-lived cookie during Google or Apple sign-in, a cookie for your light or dark theme, and local storage for your map search selections. There are no tracking cookies.
  • Apps: your sign-in tokens and map search selections are stored on your device.

Services we use

  • Railway hosts our servers and database.
  • Resend sends account emails, such as email verification.
  • Google and Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • GBIF provides public biodiversity records. We send it the map area you search, but no account data.
  • Maps are loaded from OpenStreetMap on the website, Google Maps on Android and Apple Maps on iOS. These providers can see your IP address when map tiles load.
  • Our servers keep technical logs, for example of requests and errors, to run and secure the service. They can include your email address when you sign up or sign in.

Your data, your choice

You can change your nickname and password in your profile. To get a copy of your data or to delete your account together with all your observations, email us at {{ contactEmail }}.
